------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Both runs' train_metrics.json "captions_path" field must read
  "../../outputs/semantic_captions/Caption_Train4500_Failures_587.jsonl" —

  trained on the identical pool" is a checkable fact, not an assumption.

  --predictor-init was not passed or the path is wrong.

  decrease — flat-from-epoch-1 would suggest something isn't backpropagating).

  but still needs the CI, not a raw point difference.
