Research goal

A crash warning that explains itself

The problem

Crash predictors give a score, not a reason. Today an explanation needs a second model that looks at the scene again, which doubles the cost and drifts from what the predictor actually saw.

Our model

BADAS-JEPA-Reason reads the crash-tuned V-JEPA2 video features directly and returns, in one pass: collision yes / no, its probability, the reasoning and the expected outcome.

Why it is new. V-JEPA2 has been aligned with language models for general video question answering, and BADAS-Reason explains a warning with a separate vision-language model that looks at one frame. We produce the decision and its reasoning from the same video latents, which the BADAS-2.0 authors list as future work.
